01. Trading Basics

Stock Market kya hai?

Stock Market ek marketplace hai jahan companies ke shares buy aur sell kiye jaate hain.

Important Terms

  • Stock: Company mein ownership ka ek hissa.
  • Broker: Trading karne ka platform.
  • Demat Account: Shares ko electronic form mein rakhna.
  • Trading Account: Buy aur sell orders place karna.
  • Index: Market performance dikhane wala indicator.
  • Liquidity: Kisi asset ko aasani se buy/sell kar pana.

02. Market Types

Delivery Trading

Shares ko buy karke apne Demat account mein hold karna.

Intraday Trading

Ek hi trading session mein buy aur sell karna.

Swing Trading

Kuch din ya hafton tak position hold karna.

Long-Term Investing

Mahino ya saalon tak investment hold karna.

03. Candlestick Patterns

Candlestick kya hai?

Candlestick ek fixed time period mein price ka Open, High, Low aur Close dikhati hai.
  • Green Candle: Close, Open se upar.
  • Red Candle: Close, Open se neeche.
  • Doji: Open aur Close lagbhag same.
  • Hammer: Chhoti body aur lambi lower wick.
  • Shooting Star: Chhoti body aur lambi upper wick.
  • Engulfing: Badi candle previous candle ki body ko cover karti hai.
Important: Sirf candle pattern dekhkar trade mat lena. Location, trend aur confirmation bhi check karo.

04. Trend Analysis

Uptrend 📈

Higher High (HH) + Higher Low (HL).

Downtrend 📉

Lower High (LH) + Lower Low (LL).

Sideways Market

Price ek range ke andar move karta hai.

05. Support & Resistance

Support

Woh price area jahan buying interest aane ki sambhavna hoti hai.

Resistance

Woh price area jahan selling pressure aane ki sambhavna hoti hai.

Support aur Resistance ko exact line ke bajay zone ki tarah samajhna aksar behtar hota hai.

07. Technical Indicators

RSI

Relative Strength Index momentum indicator hai. Common levels:
  • 70 ke upar: Overbought zone
  • 30 ke neeche: Oversold zone
  • 50: Momentum reference level

Moving Average

Price ka average trend samajhne mein madad karta hai.
  • 20 EMA
  • 50 EMA
  • 200 EMA

MACD

Trend aur momentum ko analyse karne ka indicator.

08. Volume Analysis

  • High Volume: Zyada trading activity.
  • Low Volume: Kam trading activity.
  • Breakout + High Volume: Breakout ko support mil sakta hai.
  • Price Rise + Falling Volume: Momentum kamzor ho sakta hai.

09. Liquidity & Liquidity Sweep

Liquidity kya hai?

Market mein woh price levels jahan orders aur stop-loss collect ho sakte hain.
  • Buy Side Liquidity: Previous highs ke upar.
  • Sell Side Liquidity: Previous lows ke neeche.
  • Liquidity Sweep: Price obvious high ya low ke paar jaakar wapas reverse ho sakta hai.

Liquidity sweep ke baad confirmation aur market structure check karna zaroori hai.

11. Risk Management

Golden Rules

  • Har trade se pehle Stop Loss decide karo.
  • Ek trade mein capital ka chhota hissa risk karo.
  • Loss recover karne ke liye revenge trading mat karo.
  • Risk/Reward ratio samjho.
  • Trading capital ko protect karna priority hai.

12. Trading Strategies

Trend + Pullback Strategy

1. Trend identify karo.
2. Support ya resistance zone mark karo.
3. Pullback ka wait karo.
4. Confirmation candle dekho.
5. Stop Loss aur Target plan karo.

Breakout Strategy

1. Important resistance identify karo.
2. Candle close ka wait karo.
3. Volume analyse karo.
4. Retest aur confirmation check karo.

Har strategy ko historical charts aur paper trading mein test karna zaroori hai.
